How to make a marriage biodata

A marriage biodata is the one-page introduction families exchange when a match is first suggested. It is not a CV. It is read quickly, usually by parents and elders, and it answers a small set of practical questions before anyone meets.

What a biodata should contain

  1. Name, date and place of birth. Time of birth matters if horoscopes will be matched.
  2. Height, and basic personal details — diet, mother tongue, marital status.
  3. Education and work. Degree, role, and the city you work in. Income is optional and many families leave it out.
  4. Family details. Parents' names and occupations, siblings, native place. For many families this section carries more weight than the career one.
  5. Horoscope details if relevant — gotra, rashi, nakshatra, manglik status.
  6. One clear contact. Usually a parent, with a phone number.

Mistakes worth avoiding

  • Too long. One page. If it runs to two, cut the adjectives, not the facts.
  • A casual photo. Plain background, natural light, looking at the camera. It does not need a studio.
  • Vague wording. "Good family values" says nothing. "Father retired from State Bank, mother is a school teacher" says a great deal.
  • Sharing it too widely. A biodata carries your full name, photo, birth details and a phone number. Send it to specific people, not open WhatsApp groups.

Which language should you use?

Use the language the receiving family reads most comfortably. Many people make two versions — one in English for wider circulation, one in the mother tongue for close relatives and community groups. You can switch language here and download again; your details are kept.

Is this really free?

Yes. Every language, every design, no watermark, no account. It is free because it costs nothing to run — the work happens in your browser, not on a server.

Where do my photo and details go?

Nowhere. Everything stays in your browser's memory and, if you save a draft, in your own device storage. Nothing is uploaded, so nobody can see it — including me.

Can I edit it later?

Yes. Your work is saved automatically on this device, and "Restore draft" brings it back. You can also download a backup file and load it on another device.

My language is not in the list.

Every label can be edited — click any label above the box and type over it. You can also add your own fields, so a biodata in any language your keyboard supports is possible.

Should I include my income?

It is optional and entirely your choice. Many families leave it out at first and discuss it later. Leave the field blank and it will not appear.

What size does it print at?

Standard A4. The PDF is set up for it, so any print shop or home printer will handle it without resizing.

Why a browser-based tool

A marriage biodata contains your full name, photograph, exact birth time, family details and a phone number. That is a lot to hand to a website you have never heard of. This one was built so the question never arises: the page does the work on your own device, and there is no server to upload to.
