Privacy policy

This is specific rather than vague, because a biodata contains unusually sensitive information.

What you enter

Your name, birth details, family details, photograph and phone number are processed entirely inside your own browser. They are never uploaded, never transmitted, and never seen by any server. There is no database here for them to be stored in.

Your photograph

The photo you add is read by your own device, resized by your own device, and drawn into the biodata by your own device. It does not travel anywhere.

Saved drafts

So that you do not lose work, your draft is saved to your browser's local storage — a private area on your own device. Clearing your browser data removes it. Anyone with access to your unlocked device could open the page and see the draft, so use the Clear button on a shared computer.

Analytics and cookies

No analytics scripts. No tracking cookies. No advertising.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the download feature loads two open-source libraries from public CDNs when you press the button. These see your IP address, as with any website loading external files. None of your biodata content is shared with them.
